Git

j shows a repository's state in a status buffer, as magit and Neogit do: what has changed, what is staged, and the recent commits, with each file's diff inline. j runs the git on your path for it, version 2.16 or later. To see the commands it runs, use vcsCommandLog ($ in the status buffer).

The status buffer

gitStatus, mapped to Ctrl Alt G, and to <leader>gg in vim edit mode, opens the status of the current file's repository. In a project whose directory holds several repositories, it shows them all, each under a heading in its own color. 

Head:     main  1a2b3c4 icons: draw at the window's device scale
Upstream: origin/main  ↑2 ↓0

Untracked files (1)
  ?  notes.txt

Unstaged changes (2)
  M  src/Display.java
  @@ -60,6 +60,12 @@ class Display
  ...
Staged changes (1)
  A  doc/git.html

Recent commits (10)
  1a2b3c4 (HEAD -> main, origin/main) icons: draw at the window's device scale

The sections are those that have something in them: unmerged files during a merge, untracked files, unstaged changes, staged changes, and the recent commits. A line under the header says when a merge, a rebase or a cherry-pick is in progress, and how many conflicts it has.

A dimmed row over the first line shows the main keys, in the notation of the edit mode you are in; vcsHints turns it off. ? opens a popup of the rest, and F1 this page.

Showing and hiding

A section, a file and a hunk each open and close: Tab on one, or on a line in it, toggles it, as does a click on the chevron in the gutter beside it. A file starts closed; opening it reads its diff. Shift Tab steps everything through four depths, and 1 to 4 go to one: 1 shows the sections, 2 their files, 3 the files' hunks, and 4 every line of the diffs. In vim edit mode za, zo and zc toggle, open and close, and zR and zM open and close everything.

In a diff, the words that changed in a line are on a stronger shade than the rest of it, when a removed line and the added line that replaces it have enough in common.

Moving and visiting

n and p go to the next and previous thing that opens, or commit; Alt N and Alt P, and ]] and [[ in vim edit mode, to the next and previous section. Enter, or a double click, visits the thing at the caret: a file, at the line of the hunk or diff line the caret is on, or a commit, shown with its diff. A staged line is visited where it is now, after the changes made since it was staged.

Refreshing

The buffer is read again when the repository's index, HEAD or branches change, when you save a file, and when j's window comes back to the front. g (r in vim edit mode, where gg goes to the top) reads it again now, for a change j could not see. Reading it again keeps the caret on the file or line it was on.

The buffer list

The buffer lists in the sidebar, and the Ctrl E switcher, show a file's status after its name: M changed, M in green staged, A added, ? untracked, D deleted, R renamed, C copied and U conflicted. gitBufferListStatus shows a dot instead, or nothing. When the project has several repositories, a strip at the left of a row is its repository's color.
